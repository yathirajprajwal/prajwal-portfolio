    Shui.AI said "shoo-ee"

    An AI teammate that joins Google Meet calls.

    I built Shui solo, end to end in .NET 10. Invite it to a call, say "Hey Shui", and it answers out loud from the team's own code, pull requests, issues and docs, with a source for every claim. When it doesn't know, it says so. When the call ends, it writes the notes.

    In-call messages

    Shui's answers and their sources show up here.

    This demo is scripted in your browser so it's instant, but every behaviour in it comes from the real product: cited answers, catch-up, the room rule and "no source, no answer". Turn the voice on to hear it.

    What happens in a call

    1. Invite

      Add Shui to a Calendar event, or use Meet's "Add people" mid-call.

    2. Join

      It joins a minute before start, on its own browser profile, several calls at once.

    3. Listen

      Reads Meet's live captions and waits for people to finish talking.

    4. Search

      Vector and keyword search over your sources, filtered by who may see what.

    5. Answer

      Speaks in a natural voice and posts the sources in the chat.

    6. Notes

      Summary, decisions and action items, indexed so later questions can cite them.

    Who it helps

    Teams losing context

    When the person who knew why something was built leaves, the answer is still in the PRs and issues. Shui finds it and points to it.

    People who joined late

    "Hey Shui, catch me up" gives a short recap of what was said, without stopping the meeting.

    Managers and leads

    Notes with decisions and owners arrive after every call. Nobody has to take minutes.

    Security and compliance

    Permissions are enforced inside the database query, every answer is audited, and in a room Shui only uses what everyone there may hear.

    What I built it with, and why

    .NET 10 · ASP.NET Core MVC
    Razor views, a JSON API and background workers in one classic .sln.
    Microsoft.Extensions.AI
    One interface for every model, so swapping providers is configuration.
    PostgreSQL + pgvector
    Hybrid search: vectors plus full-text, merged with reciprocal rank fusion.
    Free model chain
    Gemini, Groq, Cerebras, OpenRouter and Mistral free tiers, then local Ollama, tried in turn.
    Playwright
    Drives a real Edge or Chrome window into Google Meet, captions in, voice out.
    Kokoro TTS · SearXNG
    A self-hosted voice, and private web search with no API key.
    MailKit · Ical.Net
    Picks up calendar invites and Meet invitations so Shui joins by itself.
    OpenID Connect
    Google or Entra sign-in, per-user rate limits, fail-closed startup.
    Docker · Caddy · GitHub Actions
    One-command deploy on a free VM with HTTPS, and CI with 80+ xUnit tests.
